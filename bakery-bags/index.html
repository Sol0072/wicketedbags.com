Buyer's guide

Wicketed bread bags: what to know before you order.

What “on wicket” means

Wicketed bread bags are made as flat bags with a lip at the top. Two holes are punched through the lip, and a stack of bags is slid onto a U-shaped wire called a wicket. On the bakery line, the top bag is opened, the loaf goes in, and the filled bag pulls free while the next bag waits in place. The same stack works on an automatic bagger, a semi-automatic bagger, or by hand.

Getting the size right

A bread bag has to fit two things: your loaf and your bagger. When you ask for a quote, it helps to know:

  • Width and length of the bag, measured flat.
  • Gusset, if you run tall or square loaves that need extra room.
  • Lip size and hole spacing, so the bags hang right on your wicket.
  • Your machine, or how you load today.

Not sure? Send us a bag you already use, or your machine's specs, and we'll match it.

PE or PP film?

We make bread bags in both. PE (polyethylene) is soft and flexible. PP (polypropylene) is clearer and stiffer, with more shine on the shelf. Thicker film is sturdier but uses more material, so the right gauge depends on your loaf. Tell us what you bake and we'll help you choose. Our films are FDA-compliant for food contact.

Print your brand, or go plain

A bread bag is often the only label your loaf has. We flexo print up to 8 colors, from a simple one-color logo to full-color graphics, or make clear bags with no print. See custom printed wicketed bags for what to send for artwork.

Other bakery bags

Flatbreads have their own needs. We also make wicketed pita bags and wicketed tortilla bags, and you can read more about wicketed bags in general.

FAQ

Bakery bag questions.

What are wicketed bread bags?

Poly bread bags with two holes in the lip, stacked on a wire wicket. They load fast on automatic and semi-automatic bread baggers, or by hand.

Can you print our bakery logo on bread bags?

Yes. Flexo printing up to 8 colors on clear, white or metallized film.

Will your bakery bags fit our bagger?

We make them to your spec: bag size, gauge, wicket and hole spacing. Send us a sample bag or your machine's specs and we'll match it.

How do I measure a bread bag?

Measure it flat: width, length, any gusset, and the lip with its wicket hole spacing. Or just send us a bag you use now and we'll match it.

Should bread bags be PE or PP?

Both work. PE is soft and flexible. PP is clearer and stiffer, with more shine. Tell us what you bake and we'll help you choose.

What's the minimum order and lead time?

Minimums depend on size, film and print, so ask us. Typical lead time is 3–4 weeks.
