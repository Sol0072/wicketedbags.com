FAQ

Frozen food & ice bag questions.

Do you make wicketed ice bags?

Yes. Wicketed bags for packaged ice, printed or plain, made to your spec.

Do you make bags for frozen food?

Yes. Tell us what you're packing and how it's stored, and we'll recommend a film. We use FDA-compliant films for food contact.

Can frozen food and ice bags be printed?

Yes. Flexo printing up to 8 colors on clear, white or metallized film.

How long does an order take?

Typical lead time is 3–4 weeks. Minimums depend on size, film and print, so ask us.

How do I get a quote?

Fill in the quote form on this page, or email quote@wicketedbags.com with your bag size, quantity, film, structure and gauge. A sample of your current bag helps us match it exactly.
