FAQ

Produce bag questions.

Can you add vent holes to wicketed produce bags?

Yes. Vent holes are one of our standard options, placed to your spec.

What does an Anti-Fog coating do?

It helps keep condensation from fogging up the inside of the bag, so shoppers can see the produce.

Can produce bags be printed?

Yes. Flexo printing up to 8 colors, or clear bags with no print.

Are your produce bags food safe?

We use FDA-compliant films for food contact.
